Why Giorgio?

Named for Giorgio Vasari, the Renaissance artist, architect, and biographer who helped shape the telling of art history.

An old name. A new kind of company.

Art has stories.
You have questions.

The name is a nod to Vasari’s world of artists and their stories. The invitation is for everyone—not just the people who already know the names.

Ask Giorgio began with a day at the Prado and the pleasure of asking one more question. That’s the spirit: less lecture, more conversation. A little context when you want it, and room for your own curiosity.

Before you go

A few practical things.

Can Giorgio help me plan a tour?

Yes. Choose highlights, a tour, or a themed tour, then share your museum, time, and interests. Giorgio can help develop a flexible itinerary in your AI app. Current museum information and the signs in front of you guide the actual route.

What if I change my mind?

Say so. Skip a stop, follow something unexpected, or stay with one work. You can also ask for shorter answers, fewer questions, or “just tell me about it.” The plan should serve your visit, not the other way around.

Can I keep my interests for next time?

Ask for a short visitor profile, review what you want to keep, and save it for your next conversation. It’s your file to carry between visits and AI services—not automatic memory created by this website.

Are Museum Packs available?

Not yet. This experimental release provides the guide instructions, not a reviewed museum collection. Ask your AI to check current museum sources and be clear about what it cannot verify.

Do I need photos, headphones, or a connection?

Photos are optional and should follow museum rules. Headphones can make voice more comfortable; text works too. Your AI and its voice features may need a connection. Downloading the guide does not create an offline AI app.

About the guide

Designed to keep answers grounded and flag uncertainty. The guide asks your AI to use available evidence and avoid unsupported visual claims; it is an experimental draft, not a validated museum guide or museum-verified catalog. No Museum Packs are included yet.
